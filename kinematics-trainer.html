📖 Kinematics Equations

1D Linear Motion (constant $a$)

$v = u + at$
$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
$v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
$s = \frac{(u+v)}{2}t$

Freefall / Vertical Motion ($g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$)

$v = \sqrt{2gh}$ (impact speed from height $h$)
$t_{\text{total}} = \frac{2u}{g}$ (time up & down)
$h_{\max} = \frac{u^2}{2g}$ (max height)

2D Projectile Motion

$R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$ (range)
$H = \frac{(v_0 \sin\theta)^2}{2g}$ (peak height)
$t = \frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g}$ (time of flight)
$x = v_0 t$ (horizontal launch off a height)
