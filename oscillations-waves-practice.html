📐 Chapter 16 Reference Formulas

1. Hooke's Law & Elastic Potential Energy

Restoring force and elastic strain energy:

$$F_s = -kx, \quad U_s = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$$

• $k$: force/spring constant $(\text{N/m})$
• $x$: deformation from equilibrium position $(\text{m})$

2. Period, Frequency & Angular Frequency

$$f = \frac{1}{T}, \quad T = \frac{1}{f}, \quad \omega = 2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{T}$$

• $T$: period $(\text{s})$, $f$: frequency $(\text{Hz})$, $\omega$: angular frequency $(\text{rad/s})$

3. Simple Harmonic Motion (Mass-Spring System)

$$\omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}, \quad T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}, \quad f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$

Kinematics equations:

$$x(t) = A\cos(\omega_0 t + \phi), \quad v(t) = -A\omega_0\sin(\omega_0 t + \phi), \quad a(t) = -A\omega_0^2\cos(\omega_0 t + \phi) = -\omega_0^2 x$$ $$v_{\max} = A\omega_0 = A\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}, \quad |a|_{\max} = A\omega_0^2 = A\frac{k}{m}, \quad A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \left(\frac{v_0}{\omega_0}\right)^2}$$

4. Simple Pendulum

Small angular displacement limit $(\sin\theta \approx \theta)$:

$$\omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}, \quad T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}, \quad g = \frac{4\pi^2 L}{T_0^2}$$ $$A_s = L\theta_0, \quad v_{\max} \approx \theta_0\sqrt{gL}$$

5. Energy in Simple Harmonic Motion

$$E = K + U = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}kx^2 = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{1}{2}mv_{\max}^2$$ $$|v| = \omega_0\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}(A^2 - x^2)}$$

• Energy equipartition: $K = U = \frac{1}{2}E$ occurs at $x = \pm \frac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$.

6. Damped Harmonic Motion

Equation of motion: $m\ddot x + b\dot x + kx = 0$, where $\beta = \frac{b}{2m}$:

$$\text{Underdamped } (b < b_c): \quad \omega_d = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \beta^2}, \quad T_d = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_d}$$ $$\text{Critical damping: } b_c = 2\sqrt{mk}, \quad \zeta = \frac{b}{b_c} = \frac{\beta}{\omega_0}$$ $$\text{Amplitude envelope: } A_{\text{env}}(t) = A_0 e^{-\beta t}, \quad t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\beta}$$

7. Forced Oscillations & Resonance

Steady-state amplitude for driver $F_0\cos(\Omega t)$:

$$X(\Omega) = \frac{F_0}{\sqrt{(k - m\Omega^2)^2 + (b\Omega)^2}}$$ $$\text{Resonance } (\Omega = \omega_0): \quad X_{\text{res}} = \frac{F_0}{b\omega_0}, \quad Q_{\text{qual}} = \frac{m\omega_0}{b}$$ $$\text{Average absorbed power: } \langle P\rangle = \frac{1}{2}b\Omega^2 X^2$$

8. Traveling Waves & Wave Speed

$$c = \lambda f = \frac{\lambda}{T} = \frac{\omega}{\kappa}, \quad \kappa = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}, \quad \omega = 2\pi f$$

Transverse wave speed on a stretched flexible string (tension $\mathcal T$, linear mass density $\mu$):

$$c = \sqrt{\frac{\mathcal T}{\mu}}, \quad v_{y,\max} = A\omega$$

9. Interference, Standing Waves & Beats

Resultant amplitude of two coherent waves with phase difference $\Delta\phi$:

$$A_R = \sqrt{A_1^2 + A_2^2 + 2A_1 A_2 \cos\Delta\phi} \quad \xrightarrow{A_1=A_2=A} \quad A_R = 2A\left|\cos\frac{\Delta\phi}{2}\right|$$

Standing waves on string fixed at both ends (length $L$):

$$\lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}, \quad f_n = \frac{nc}{2L} = n f_1 \quad (n = 1, 2, 3, \dots)$$

Beat frequency (superposition of two nearby frequencies $f_1, f_2$):

$$f_{\text{beat}} = |f_1 - f_2|, \quad f_{\text{avg}} = \frac{f_1 + f_2}{2}, \quad T_{\text{beat}} = \frac{1}{f_{\text{beat}}}$$

10. Wave Energy, Power & Intensity

Average power transmitted along a stretched string:

$$\langle P\rangle = \frac{1}{2}\mu\omega^2 A^2 c$$

Intensity and 3D spherical inverse-square spreading:

$$I = \frac{P}{\mathcal A}, \quad I(r) = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}, \quad \frac{I_1}{I_2} = \left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^2$$ $$E = I\mathcal A\Delta t, \quad P_{\text{detected}} = I\mathcal A_{\text{detector}}$$
