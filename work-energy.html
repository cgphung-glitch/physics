Chapter 7 Formula Reference

Work & Net Work

Work by Constant Force: $W = Fd \cos \theta$

Work-Energy Theorem: $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta K = K_f - K_i$

Kinetic & Potential Energy

Kinetic Energy: $\displaystyle K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$

Gravitational Potential Energy: $\Delta U_g = m g \Delta y$

Hooke's Law Spring Potential: $\displaystyle U_s = \frac{1}{2} k x^2$

Conservation & Friction

Energy with Nonconservative Work: $K_i + U_i + W_{\text{nc}} = K_f + U_f$

Frictional Work: $W_f = -f_k d = -\mu_k N d$

Power, Efficiency & Energy

Average Power: $\displaystyle P = \frac{\Delta E}{\Delta t} = F v$

Efficiency: $\displaystyle \eta = \frac{\text{Useful Output}}{\text{Total Input}} \times 100\%$

Electrical Energy (kWh): $E(\text{kWh}) = P(\text{kW}) \times t(\text{hours})$
