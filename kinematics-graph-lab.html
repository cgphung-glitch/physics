Drag the amber points on the velocity graph up or down. Position and acceleration update instantly: acceleration is the slope of each segment, and position is the running total (integral) of velocity.
• A flat segment on v(t) → zero acceleration.
• A rising segment → positive acceleration.
• A point below the axis → moving in the negative direction.
