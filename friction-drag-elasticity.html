Friction

Box Pushed at an Angle

Use $g = 9.80\text{ m/s}^2$

Step-by-Step Worked Solution

5 Reference Formula Sheet

Governing equations, constants, & material properties

1. Friction Equations

Static Friction Maximum

$$f_{s,\text{max}} = \mu_s N$$

Slipping threshold: $f_s \le \mu_s N$

Kinetic Friction

$$f_k = \mu_k N$$

Active sliding friction force ($\mu_k < \mu_s$)

Pushing Below Horizontal ($\theta$)

$$N = mg + F\sin\theta$$ $$F_{\text{min}} = \frac{\mu_s mg}{\cos\theta - \mu_s\sin\theta}$$

Pulling Above Horizontal ($\theta$)

$$N = mg - F\sin\theta$$ $$F_{\text{min}} = \frac{\mu_s mg}{\cos\theta + \mu_s\sin\theta}$$

Incline Ramp ($\theta$ Angle)

$$N = mg\cos\theta, \quad F_{\parallel} = mg\sin\theta, \quad a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta)$$

2. Drag & Fluid Resistance

Turbulent Drag Force

$$F_d = \frac{1}{2} C_d \rho A v^2$$

$C_d$: drag coeff, $\rho$: fluid density, $A$: area

Power to Overcome Drag

$$P = F_d \cdot v = \frac{1}{2} C_d \rho A v^3$$

Power scales cubicly with speed ($P \propto v^3$)

Turbulent Terminal Velocity

$$v_t = \sqrt{\frac{2mg}{C_d \rho A}}$$

Occurs when drag equals weight ($F_d = mg$)

Stokes' Law (Low Speed Spheres)

$$F_d = 6\pi r \eta v, \quad v_t = \frac{2(\rho_p - \rho_f) r^2 g}{9\eta}$$

$\eta$: dynamic viscosity, $r$: radius

3. Elasticity & Mechanical Properties

Tensile Stress & Strain

$$\text{Stress } \sigma = \frac{F}{A}, \quad \text{Strain } \epsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L_0}$$

Young's Modulus ($Y$)

$$Y = \frac{\text{Stress}}{\text{Strain}} = \frac{F/A}{\Delta L / L_0} \implies \Delta L = \frac{F L_0}{A Y}$$

Shear Deformation ($S$)

$$\text{Shear Stress } \tau = \frac{F}{A}, \quad \gamma = \frac{\tau}{S}$$

Bulk Compression ($B$)

$$\Delta P = -B \left(\frac{\Delta V}{V_0}\right) \implies F = \Delta P \cdot A$$

4. Standard Physical Constants & Material Moduli

Environmental Constants

  • • Gravity $g = 9.80\text{ m/s}^2$
  • • Air density $\rho_{\text{air}} = 1.20\text{ kg/m}^3$ (or $1.21$)
  • • Water density $\rho_{\text{water}} = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$
  • • Water viscosity $\eta = 1.00 \times 10^{-3}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$

Elastic Moduli Values

  • • Steel Young's Modulus: $Y_{\text{steel}} = 2.0 \times 10^{11}\text{ Pa}$
  • • Steel Shear Modulus: $S_{\text{steel}} = 8.0 \times 10^{10}\text{ Pa}$
  • • Nylon Young's Modulus: $Y_{\text{nylon}} = 3.7 \times 10^{9}\text{ Pa}$
  • • Water Bulk Modulus: $B_{\text{water}} = 2.20 \times 10^{9}\text{ Pa}$
