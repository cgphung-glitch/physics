📐 Chapter 15 Reference Formulas

1. First Law of Thermodynamics

Closed system energy accounting (system-work convention):

$$\Delta U = Q - W$$

• $Q > 0$: heat enters system; $W > 0$: work done by system on surroundings.
• Note: chemistry convention uses $w = -W$, giving $\Delta U = q + w$.

2. Work & Ideal Gas Specific Heats

Pressure-volume work:

$$W = \int_{V_i}^{V_f} p_{\text{ext}}\,dV \quad \xrightarrow{\text{isobaric}} \quad W = p\,\Delta V = nR\,\Delta T$$

Monatomic ideal gas capacities:

$$C_V = \frac{3}{2}R, \quad C_P = \frac{5}{2}R, \quad \gamma = \frac{C_P}{C_V} = \frac{5}{3} \approx 1.667, \quad \Delta U = nC_V\Delta T$$ $$R = 8.314\,\text{J}/(\text{mol}\cdot\text{K})$$

3. Specialized Ideal Gas Processes

Isochoric ($V = \text{const}$): $W = 0, \; Q = \Delta U = nC_V\Delta T$

Reversible Isothermal ($T = \text{const}$): $\Delta U = 0, \; Q = W = nRT \ln\left(\frac{V_f}{V_i}\right)$

Reversible Adiabatic ($Q = 0$):

$$pV^\gamma = \text{const}, \quad TV^{\gamma-1} = \text{const}, \quad W = -\Delta U = \frac{p_iV_i - p_fV_f}{\gamma - 1} = nC_V(T_i - T_f)$$

Rectangular $p$-$V$ Cycle: $W_{\text{net}} = (p_h - p_l)(V_2 - V_1)$ (clockwise = positive work)

4. Heat Engines & Otto Cycle

$$\Delta U_{\text{cycle}} = 0 \implies W_{\text{out}} = Q_h - Q_c$$ $$\eta = \frac{W_{\text{out}}}{Q_h} = 1 - \frac{Q_c}{Q_h}$$

Ideal Otto Cycle (compression ratio $r = V_1/V_2$):

$$\eta_{\text{Otto}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{\gamma-1}}$$

5. Carnot Limit & Second Law

Maximum possible efficiency between reservoirs $T_h$ and $T_c$ (Kelvin absolute temperatures):

$$\eta_{\text{C}} = 1 - \frac{T_c}{T_h} = \frac{T_h - T_c}{T_h}, \quad \frac{Q_c}{Q_h} = \frac{T_c}{T_h}$$

6. Heat Pumps & Refrigerators (COP)

$$Q_h = Q_c + W_{\text{in}}$$ $$\text{COP}_{\text{R}} = \frac{Q_c}{W_{\text{in}}}, \quad \text{COP}_{\text{HP}} = \frac{Q_h}{W_{\text{in}}} = \text{COP}_{\text{R}} + 1$$

Carnot theoretical upper limits:

$$\text{COP}_{\text{R,C}} = \frac{T_c}{T_h - T_c}, \quad \text{COP}_{\text{HP,C}} = \frac{T_h}{T_h - T_c}$$

7. Entropy, Phase Transitions & Exergy

$$\Delta S = \int \frac{\delta Q_{\text{rev}}}{T}$$

• Isothermal reservoir transfer: $\Delta S = \frac{Q}{T}$

• Heating body ($c = \text{const}$): $\Delta S = mc\ln\left(\frac{T_f}{T_i}\right)$

• Phase change ($L_f = 334{,}000\,\text{J/kg}$): $\Delta S = \frac{mL}{T_{\text{tr}}}$

• Ideal gas: $\Delta S = nC_V\ln\left(\frac{T_f}{T_i}\right) + nR\ln\left(\frac{V_f}{V_i}\right)$

• Lost work potential (Gouy-Stodola): $W_{\text{lost}} = T_0 S_{\text{gen}}$

8. Statistical Multiplicity

$$S = k_B \ln \Omega, \quad k_B = 1.380649\times 10^{-23}\,\text{J/K}$$ $$\Omega(h) = \binom{N}{h} = \frac{N!}{h!(N-h)!}, \quad P(h) = \frac{\Omega(h)}{2^N}$$
