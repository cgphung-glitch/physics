Projectile Motion Reference

Launch angle θ is measured above the horizontal; the ground is y = 0 and the launch height is h₀. Air resistance is ignored.

Launch components
v₀ₓ = v₀ cos θ
v₀ᵧ = v₀ sin θ
Position at time t
x(t) = v₀ₓ t
y(t) = h₀ + v₀ᵧ t − ½ g t²
Velocity at time t
vₓ(t) = v₀ₓ (constant)
vᵧ(t) = v₀ᵧ − g t
v = √(vₓ² + vᵧ²)
direction = tan⁻¹(vᵧ / vₓ)
Peak of the path
tpeak = v₀ᵧ / g
ymax = h₀ + v₀ᵧ² / (2g)
Flight time and range
tf = [v₀ᵧ + √(v₀ᵧ² + 2 g h₀)] / g
(solves y = 0)
R = v₀ₓ tf
Impact velocity (landing)
vₓ,f = v₀ₓ
vᵧ,f = v₀ᵧ − g tf = −√(v₀ᵧ² + 2 g h₀)
vf = √(vₓ,f² + vᵧ,f²) = √(v₀² + 2 g h₀)
angle below horizontal = tan⁻¹(|vᵧ,f| / vₓ,f)
Level ground (no launch height)
tf = 2 v₀ sin θ / g
R = v₀² sin 2θ / g
ymax = v₀² sin² θ / (2g)
Maximum range at θ = 45°; angles θ and 90° − θ give the same range; vf = v₀.

Moon 1.62 m/s²

or any custom value in the sandbox
