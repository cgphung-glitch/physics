1. Vector Components & Magnitude

$$A_x = A \cos\theta, \quad A_y = A \sin\theta$$

$$A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2}, \quad \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{|A_y|}{|A_x|}\right)$$

2. 2D Projectile Kinematics ($g = 9.80\text{ m/s}^2$)

$$x = (v_0 \cos\theta) t$$

$$y = (v_0 \sin\theta) t - \frac{1}{2}g t^2$$

$$v_x = v_0 \cos\theta, \quad v_y = v_0 \sin\theta - g t$$

3. Symmetric Ground Launch ($y_0 = y_f = 0$)

$$t_{\text{flight}} = \frac{2 v_0 \sin\theta}{g}, \quad h_{\max} = \frac{(v_0 \sin\theta)^2}{2g}$$

$$R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$$
