Kinematics Demos

Constant velocity, constant acceleration, and piecewise acceleration & Δv — three linked motion explorers in one lab notebook.

Motion with constant velocity follows x(t) = x₀ + v·t. Adjust the sliders and watch the slope and intercept of the graph respond.

x₀ = 0 m
v = 2 m/s
t = 5 s

x(t) = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² and v(t) = v₀ + at, plotted together. Interactions are logged for instructor review.

v₀ = 2 m/s
a = 1 m/s²
t = 5 s
Slider interactions post a kinematicsDemoEvent message to the parent window for activity tracking.

Two constant-acceleration phases: a₁ for time T₁, then a₂ for time T₂. The shaded area under a(t) in each interval equals the change in velocity for that interval.

a₁ = 2 m/s²
T₁ = 4 s
a₂ = -1 m/s²
T₂ = 4 s
