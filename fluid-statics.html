Reference formulas
  • $\rho = m/V$  ·  $P = F/A$  ·  $P = P_0 + \rho g h$
  • $F_B = \rho_{\text{fluid}} g V_{\text{disp}}$  ·  floating: $F_B = mg$
  • Hydraulic: $F_1/A_1 = F_2/A_2$  ·  $A_1 d_1 = A_2 d_2$
  • Manometer: $P_g = \rho g h$  ·  $1\ \text{mm Hg} = 133.3\ \text{Pa}$
  • Surface tension: $F = \gamma L$ (film: $2\gamma L$)  ·  bubble: $\Delta P = 4\gamma/r$
  • Capillary: $h = \frac{2\gamma\cos\theta}{\rho g r}$  ·  $P_g = \rho g h$
  • Absolute: $P_{\text{abs}} = P_{\text{atm}} + P_g$  ·  $P_{\text{atm}} = 1.013\times10^5\ \text{Pa}$
