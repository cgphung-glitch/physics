📐 Chapter 20 Reference Formulas & Constants

1. Fundamental Physical Constants

$$\begin{aligned} e &= 1.602 \times 10^{-19}\,\text{C} \\ N_A &= 6.022 \times 10^{23}\,\text{mol}^{-1} \\ \varepsilon_0 &\approx 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\,\text{F/m} \end{aligned}$$

2. Electric Current & Carrier Drift

$$I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}, \quad i(t) = \frac{dQ}{dt}, \quad \dot N = \frac{|I|}{z e}$$ $$|I| = n |q| A v_d \quad \implies \quad v_d = \frac{I}{n e A} \quad (A = \frac{\pi D^2}{4})$$

3. Ohm's Law & Simple Circuits

$$V = I R, \quad I = \frac{V}{R}, \quad R = \frac{V}{I}$$

• Single-loop series resistance: $R_{\text{total}} = R_L + R_{\text{line}}$

4. Resistance, Resistivity & Temperature

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{4\rho L}{\pi D^2}, \quad \text{Stretched wire (constant Vol): } R_f = s^2 R_i$$ $$R(T) = R_0 [1 + \alpha(T - T_0)], \quad T = T_0 + \frac{R/R_0 - 1}{\alpha}$$

5. Electric Power & Energy Economics

$$P = V I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}, \quad \mathcal{E} = P \cdot t$$ $$1\,\text{kWh} = 3.60 \times 10^6\,\text{J}, \quad \text{Cost} = \mathcal{E}_{\text{kWh}} \times \text{Rate}_{\$/\text{kWh}}$$

6. Alternating Current & High-Voltage Transmission

$$V_{\text{rms}} = \frac{V_0}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad I_{\text{rms}} = \frac{I_0}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad P_{\text{avg}} = V_{\text{rms}} I_{\text{rms}} = \frac{V_{\text{rms}}^2}{R}$$ $$\text{Line power loss: } P_{\text{loss}} = I^2 R_{\text{line}} = \frac{P^2 R_{\text{line}}}{V_{\text{trans}}^2}$$

7. Electrical Safety & Biological Current

$$\text{Degraded contact heating: } P_{\text{contact}} = I^2 R_{\text{contact}}, \quad \Delta T = \frac{P t}{m c}$$ $$\text{Axon membrane field: } E = \frac{|V_m|}{d}, \quad \text{Patch charge: } \Delta Q = C_m \Delta V_m$$ $$\text{ECG Limb-Lead Identity: } V_{II} = V_I + V_{III}, \quad \text{Heart Rate (BPM)} = \frac{60}{T_{RR}}$$
